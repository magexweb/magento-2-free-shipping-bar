# Free Shipping Bar for Magento 2

Turn your free shipping policy into a real-time, on-brand incentive with a live progress bar that updates instantly as shoppers add to cart — no coding needed.

---

## 📖 Detailed Explanation

Most shoppers never notice a store's free shipping policy until they hit checkout — by then it's too late to influence their cart. **Free Shipping Bar** solves this by placing a dynamic notification bar directly on the storefront, so the incentive works while customers are still browsing and adding items, not after.

The bar automatically compares each shopper's current cart subtotal against the free-shipping threshold you set, and displays one of three fully customizable messages:

- **Initial message** – invites the shopper to start shopping toward free shipping.
- **Progress message** – tells them exactly how much more they need to spend, updating live.
- **Success message** – confirms once free shipping has been unlocked.

Everything updates instantly via **AJAX** the moment a product is added to the cart — no page refresh required.

Core capabilities include:

- **Live AJAX Progress Bar** – A real-time visual progress track with an optional percentage badge (e.g., "65% there" / "Unlocked!") that grows as the cart total increases.
- **Per-Customer-Group Thresholds** – Set a different free shipping amount and messaging for Guests, General customers, Wholesale accounts, or any custom customer group via a dedicated thresholds grid.
- **Three Customizable Message States** – Write your own initial, in-progress, and success messages using live variables like remaining amount and cart total.
- **Flexible Placement & Behavior** – Choose from 4 display positions, make the bar sticky while scrolling, set an auto-hide timer, or add a close button.
- **Page-Level Display Control** – Show the bar only on the pages that matter — Home, Category, Product, Search Results, Cart, Checkout, or CMS pages.
- **Full Design Customization** – Control text alignment, font size, background/text/value colors, progress bar and badge colors, plus a custom CSS field for advanced styling.

The extension is compatible with **Magento Open Source 2.4.8-p5 and 2.4.9**, is configured entirely from the Magento Admin Panel (**Stores > Configuration > Magexweb Extensions > Free Shipping Bar Configuration**), and requires no custom development.

> **Note:** The bar is a notification and marketing tool — it does not apply the free shipping discount itself. You'll still need an active Free Shipping method or cart price rule in Magento with a matching minimum order amount, so the bar's threshold should mirror that rule for accurate messaging.

## 💡 Why It's Useful for a Magento 2 Website

1. **Influences Shoppers While It Still Matters** – Free shipping information is normally invisible until checkout. Showing it live, during browsing and cart-building, actually shapes purchase behavior instead of just confirming it after the fact.
2. **Increases Average Order Value (AOV)** – A visible "spend $X more for free shipping" message is one of the most proven, low-effort tactics for nudging shoppers to add one more item to their cart.
3. **Reduces Cart Abandonment from Shipping Surprises** – Unexpected shipping costs are a leading cause of abandoned carts. A live progress bar sets expectations early and removes checkout-stage surprises.
4. **Personalized Incentives Per Customer Segment** – Different thresholds for Guests, General, and Wholesale customers let you tailor the incentive to each segment's typical order value instead of a one-size-fits-all message.
5. **No Performance Trade-Off** – AJAX-based updates mean the bar refreshes instantly without reloading the page, keeping the shopping experience smooth.
6. **Brand-Consistent Presentation** – Full control over colors, fonts, position, and custom CSS means the bar looks like a native part of your storefront, not a bolted-on plugin.
7. **Targeted Placement** – Page-level display control ensures the bar appears only where it's useful (e.g., category and cart pages) rather than cluttering every page of the store.
8. **Zero Developer Dependency** – Marketing and store admins can adjust thresholds, messages, and design entirely from the admin panel whenever promotions or shipping policies change.

In short, this extension takes a policy most stores already have — free shipping — and turns it into an active, real-time conversion lever instead of a passive line item buried in the footer.

## ❓ FAQ

**Does this extension actually apply the free shipping discount at checkout?**
No — the bar is a notification and marketing tool. You'll still need an active Free Shipping method or cart price rule in Magento with a matching minimum order amount, mirrored in the bar's threshold setting.

**Can I set a different free shipping threshold for wholesale or logged-in customers?**
Yes. The Thresholds grid lets you add a separate row per customer group, each with its own amount, initial message, and progress message.

**Will the bar update without the customer reloading the page?**
Yes, the message and progress bar update instantly via AJAX whenever a product is added to the cart.

**Can I control which pages show the bar?**
Yes. Use the Display on Page setting to choose from Home, Category, Product, Search Results, Cart, Checkout, and CMS pages individually.

**Can shoppers dismiss the bar?**
Yes, if you enable the Close Button option. You can also set an auto-hide timer so the bar disappears on its own after a set number of seconds.

**Can I match the bar to my store's branding?**
Yes. You can fully customize text alignment, font size, background/text/value colors, progress bar and badge colors, and add custom CSS for anything beyond the built-in options.

**Is coding knowledge required to set this up?**
No. All configuration — thresholds, messages, positions, and design — is handled from the Magento Admin Panel.
